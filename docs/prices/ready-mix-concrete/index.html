Price of ready-mix concrete per cubic metre in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, Ready-mix C25/30, delivered Abuja (ex-pump) costs about ₦165,000 per m³ in Abuja (market range ₦132,000 to ₦198,000). Other lines: Ready-mix C20/25, delivered Abuja (ex-pump): ₦150,000 per m³; Ready-mix C30/37, delivered Abuja (ex-pump): ₦178,000 per m³; Ready-mix C35/45, delivered Abuja (ex-pump): ₦192,000 per m³; Ready-mix C40/50, delivered Abuja (ex-pump): ₦210,000 per m³.

What affects the price

Ready-mix is priced per cubic metre by strength grade, plus pumping. Small pours cost more per cubic metre because of minimum loads and pump charges.
