What affects the price

Older CAT D8H/D8K and 966 machines are still traded widely in Nigeria. New Chinese dozers and loaders (Shantui, Zoomlion, LiuGong, SDLG) cost less and come with a dealer warranty. On used machines, check the undercarriage, final drives, transmission and engine blow-by.
