Construction labour rates in Nigeria: daily pay by trade

As at 29 September 2026, a mason in Abuja earns about ₦15,000 a day, a labourer about ₦7,000, a carpenter about ₦17,000 and an electrician about ₦18,500. Buildocrat's all-in rates add 31% for feeding, transport, idle time, statutory costs and tools. Frequently asked questions

How much is a bricklayer (mason) paid per day in Nigeria?

About ₦15,000 a day in Abuja (range ₦12,000 to ₦18,000) as at 29 September 2026; about ₦16,200 in Lagos.

How much is a labourer paid per day in Nigeria?

About ₦7,000 a day in Abuja (range ₦5,000 to ₦8,000).

How much do carpenters, iron benders and tilers charge per day?

In Abuja: carpenter about ₦17,000, iron bender about ₦15,000, tiler about ₦15,000, electrician about ₦18,500 and plumber about ₦17,000 per day.

What is the 'all-in' labour rate?

The daily wage plus 31% for feeding, transport, non-productive time, statutory cover and small tools. It is the true cost to a contractor, and the rate used in Buildocrat's BoQ build-ups.

How much is a site engineer paid in Nigeria?

Buildocrat uses ₦450,000 a month for a COREN-registered site engineer in Abuja, before on-costs of 25.3% (pension, NSITF, ITF, HMO and leave).
