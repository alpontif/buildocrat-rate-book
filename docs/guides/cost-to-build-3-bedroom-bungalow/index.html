How much does it cost to build a 3-bedroom bungalow in Nigeria?

Using Buildocrat's rates as at 29 September 2026, the main building works for a typical 140 m² three-bedroom bungalow in Abuja come to about ₦51,584,342 (about ₦368,460 per m²) including preliminaries, overheads, contingency, profit and VAT. This covers substructure, blockwork, roof, plastering, screed, floor and wall tiles, ceilings, painting, doors, windows and basic electrical and plumbing. It excludes land, approvals, professional fees, external works, fencing, borehole, septic tank and furnishings.

Important: the quantities below are for an illustrative plan, not your drawings. Use them to budget and compare quotes, then get a bill of quantities measured from your own design.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build a 3-bedroom bungalow in Nigeria?

About ₦51,584,342 in Abuja for the main building works of a typical 140 m² bungalow as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦368,460 per m², including overheads, profit and VAT but excluding land, fees, external works and borehole (Buildocrat estimate).

What is the cost of building per square metre in Nigeria?

For a standard-finish bungalow, about ₦368,460 per m² in Abuja on Buildocrat's Sep 2026 rates. Higher finishes, suspended floors and full MEP increase this.

What is excluded?

Land, survey and approvals, professional fees, external works, fencing, borehole, septic tank, landscaping, air-conditioning and furnishings.
