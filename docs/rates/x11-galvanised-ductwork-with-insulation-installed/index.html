Frequently asked questions

How much does galvanised ductwork with insulation installed cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦35,492 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦47,372 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦37,692 per m² and the tender rate about ₦50,309 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (Expansion anchor bolt M12, Galvanised sheet ductwork (fabricated), Glass/rock wool insulation 50mm) with waste allowances; labour (AC / HVAC technician, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
