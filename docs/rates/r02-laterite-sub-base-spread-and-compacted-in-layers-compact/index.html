Frequently asked questions

How much does laterite sub-base, spread & compacted in layers (compacted volume) cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦13,628 per m³ net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦18,189 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦14,415 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦19,240 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Laterite, Water) with waste allowances; labour (Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Motor grader (CAT 140 class), Vibratory roller 10-12t, Water tanker 10,000L) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
