Aluminothermic rail weld: cost per no. in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for aluminothermic rail weld is ₦423,478 per no. in Abuja: ₦364,667 materials, ₦11,544 labour and ₦47,266 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦565,236 per no..

Frequently asked questions

How much does aluminothermic rail weld cost per no. in Nigeria?

About ₦423,478 per no. net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦565,236 per no. as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦448,645 per no. and the tender rate about ₦598,828 per no..

What does the rate include?

Materials (Aluminothermic (thermite) rail weld kit) with waste allowances; labour (Rail track worker, Rail welder (thermite)) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Rail welding & grinding unit) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
