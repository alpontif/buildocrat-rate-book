Aluminium-framed glass skylight installed and sealed: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for aluminium-framed glass skylight installed and sealed is ₦173,076 per m² in Abuja: ₦164,725 materials, ₦8,351 labour and ₦0 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦231,013 per m².

Frequently asked questions

How much does aluminium-framed glass skylight installed and sealed cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦173,076 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦231,013 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦183,628 per m² and the tender rate about ₦245,097 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (Aluminium-framed glass skylight, Polyurethane joint sealant) with waste allowances; labour (Aluminium fabricator / glazier, Unskilled labourer, Waterproofing applicator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
