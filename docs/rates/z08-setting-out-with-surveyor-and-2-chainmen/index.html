Setting out with surveyor and 2 chainmen: cost per day in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for setting out with surveyor and 2 chainmen is ₦65,237 per day in Abuja: ₦0 materials, ₦40,237 labour and ₦25,000 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦87,075 per day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does setting out with surveyor and 2 chainmen cost per day in Nigeria?

About ₦65,237 per day net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦87,075 per day as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦69,706 per day and the tender rate about ₦93,040 per day.

What does the rate include?

Materials (no materials) with waste allowances; labour (Chainman / survey assistant, Land surveyor) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Total station) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
