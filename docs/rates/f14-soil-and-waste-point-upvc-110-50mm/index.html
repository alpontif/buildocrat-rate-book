Frequently asked questions

How much does soil & waste point, uPVC 110/50mm cost per point in Nigeria?

About ₦25,300 per point net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦33,769 per point as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (uPVC 110mm bend/junction, uPVC soil pipe 110mm, uPVC waste pipe 50mm) with waste allowances; labour (Plumber, Plumber's mate) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator.
