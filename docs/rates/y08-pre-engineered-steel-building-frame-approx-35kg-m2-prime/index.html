Frequently asked questions

How much does pre-engineered steel building frame (approx. 35kg/m2), primed and erected cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦64,899 per m² net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦86,623 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦68,870 per m² and the tender rate about ₦91,925 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (Bolt & nut M16x100 grade 8.8 with washers, Red oxide anti-rust primer, Structural steel sections (UB/UC/PFC/angle), Welding electrodes E6013 2.5mm) with waste allowances; labour (Steel erector, Unskilled labourer, Welder / fabricator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t, Welding generator 400A (diesel)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Per m2 of floor area. Cladding and foundations excluded.
