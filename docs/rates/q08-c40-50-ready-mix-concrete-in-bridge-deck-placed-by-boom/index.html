Frequently asked questions

How much does c40/50 ready-mix concrete in bridge deck placed by boom pump cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦229,052 per m³ net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦305,726 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦242,837 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦324,126 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Ready-mix C40/50) with waste allowances; labour (Concrete finisher (power float), Mason / bricklayer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Concrete boom pump 37m, Poker vibrator (petrol)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Excl. rebar and formwork.
