Reinforced concrete 1:1.5:3 (~C25) in suspended slabs & beams: cost per m³ in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for reinforced concrete 1:1.5:3 (~C25) in suspended slabs & beams is ₦155,437 per m³ in Abuja: ₦129,298 materials, ₦19,896 labour and ₦6,243 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦207,469 per m³.

Frequently asked questions

How much does reinforced concrete 1:1.5:3 (~C25) in suspended slabs & beams cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦155,437 per m³ net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦207,469 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦165,099 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦220,365 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Granite 3/4", OPC 42.5 cement, Sharp sand, Water) with waste allowances; labour (Mason / bricklayer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Concrete mixer, 1-bag (petrol), Poker vibrator (petrol)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Excl. rebar & formwork.
