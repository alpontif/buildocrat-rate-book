Frequently asked questions

How much does asphalt binder course 60mm, laid & compacted cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦23,297 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦31,096 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦24,685 per m² and the tender rate about ₦32,948 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (Asphalt concrete binder course) with waste allowances; labour (Asphalt crew hand (raker/lute)) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Asphalt paver (tracked), Pneumatic-tyred roller, Tandem asphalt roller 3-8t, Tipper truck 20t) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
