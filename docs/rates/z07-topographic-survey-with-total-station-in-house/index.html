Topographic survey with total station (in-house): cost per ha in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for topographic survey with total station (in-house) is ₦19,571 per ha in Abuja: ₦0 materials, ₦12,071 labour and ₦7,500 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦26,122 per ha.

Frequently asked questions

How much does topographic survey with total station (in-house) cost per ha in Nigeria?

About ₦19,571 per ha net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦26,122 per ha as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦20,912 per ha and the tender rate about ₦27,912 per ha.

What does the rate include?

Materials (no materials) with waste allowances; labour (Chainman / survey assistant, Land surveyor) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Total station) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
