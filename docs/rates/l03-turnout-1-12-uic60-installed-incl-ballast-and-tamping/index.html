Frequently asked questions

How much does turnout 1:12 UIC60 installed incl. ballast and tamping cost per unit in Nigeria?

About ₦250,015,800 per unit net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦333,707,808 per unit as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Track ballast 25-50mm crushed granite, Turnout 1:12 UIC60 complete set) with waste allowances; labour (Rail track worker, Rail welder (thermite)) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Ballast tamping machine, Mobile crane 25t) including fuel and operator.
