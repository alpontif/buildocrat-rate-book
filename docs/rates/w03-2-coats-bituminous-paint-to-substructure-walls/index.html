2 coats bituminous paint to substructure walls: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for 2 coats bituminous paint to substructure walls is ₦1,616 per m² in Abuja: ₦1,444 materials, ₦172 labour and ₦0 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦2,157 per m².

Frequently asked questions

How much does 2 coats bituminous paint to substructure walls cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦1,616 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦2,157 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Bituminous paint) with waste allowances; labour (Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
