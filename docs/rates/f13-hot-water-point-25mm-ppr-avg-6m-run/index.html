Frequently asked questions

How much does hot water point, 25mm PPR (avg 6m run) cost per point in Nigeria?

About ₦19,579 per point net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦26,134 per point as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (PPR fittings allowance, PPR pipe 25mm) with waste allowances; labour (Plumber, Plumber's mate) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
