Frequently asked questions

How much does epoxy-coated reinforcement cut, bent and fixed cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦1,526,840 per tonne net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦2,037,945 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Binding wire, Epoxy-coated high-yield rebar) with waste allowances; labour (Iron bender / steel fixer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
