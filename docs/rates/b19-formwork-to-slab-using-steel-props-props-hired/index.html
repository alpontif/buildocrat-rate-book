Formwork to slab using steel props (props hired): cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for formwork to slab using steel props (props hired) is ₦10,545 per m² in Abuja: ₦3,958 materials, ₦4,487 labour and ₦2,100 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦14,075 per m².

Frequently asked questions

How much does formwork to slab using steel props (props hired) cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦10,545 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦14,075 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦11,246 per m² and the tender rate about ₦15,011 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (2"x4"x12' hardwood (Gmelina/Akpontu), Marine board 18mm, Wire nails) with waste allowances; labour (Carpenter (formwork & roof), Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Adjustable steel props, per 100 nr) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
