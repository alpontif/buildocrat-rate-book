Crushed stone base course, compacted (compacted volume): cost per m³ in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for crushed stone base course, compacted (compacted volume) is ₦34,666 per m³ in Abuja: ₦30,608 materials, ₦287 labour and ₦3,772 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦46,271 per m³.

Frequently asked questions

How much does crushed stone base course, compacted (compacted volume) cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦34,666 per m³ net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦46,271 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦36,714 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦49,004 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Crushed stone base 0/40 (graded), Water) with waste allowances; labour (Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Motor grader (CAT 140 class), Vibratory roller 10-12t, Water tanker 10,000L) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
