Frequently asked questions

How much does 110mm uPVC underground drain on sand bed (excl. trench) cost per m in Nigeria?

About ₦5,088 per m net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦6,792 per m as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Materials (Sharp sand, uPVC soil pipe 110mm) with waste allowances; labour (Pipe layer (drainage), Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator.
