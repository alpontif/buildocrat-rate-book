Frequently asked questions

How much does pot bearing 2,000kN supplied, installed and grouted cost per unit in Nigeria?

About ₦7,277,811 per unit net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦9,714,035 per unit as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Non-shrink cementitious grout, Pot bearing 2) with waste allowances; labour (Rigger / banksman, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
