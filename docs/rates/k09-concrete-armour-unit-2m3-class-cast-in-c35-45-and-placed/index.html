Frequently asked questions

How much does concrete armour unit (2m3 class) cast in C35/45 and placed cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦268,675 per m³ net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦358,614 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦284,436 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦379,650 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Marine board 18mm, Ready-mix C35/45) with waste allowances; labour (Carpenter (formwork & roof), Mason / bricklayer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 80t, Poker vibrator (petrol)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
