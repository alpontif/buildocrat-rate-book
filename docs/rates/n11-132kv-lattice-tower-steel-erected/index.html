Frequently asked questions

How much does 132kV lattice tower steel erected cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦4,050,099 per tonne net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦5,405,857 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Galvanised steel lattice transmission tower (132kV)) with waste allowances; labour (Rigger / banksman, Steel erector, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t) including fuel and operator.
