Clear site by bulldozer incl. grubbing roots: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for clear site by bulldozer incl. grubbing roots is ₦120 per m² in Abuja: ₦0 materials, ₦11.46 labour and ₦109 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦160 per m².

Frequently asked questions

How much does clear site by bulldozer incl. grubbing roots cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦120 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦160 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦126 per m² and the tender rate about ₦169 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (no materials) with waste allowances; labour (Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Bulldozer D6 class) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
