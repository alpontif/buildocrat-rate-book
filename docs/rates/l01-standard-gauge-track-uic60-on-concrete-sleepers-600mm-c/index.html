Standard-gauge track UIC60 on concrete sleepers (600mm c/c) incl. ballast, sub-ballast, fastenings and welds: cost per m in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for standard-gauge track UIC60 on concrete sleepers (600mm c/c) incl. ballast, sub-ballast, fastenings and welds is ₦607,483 per m in Abuja: ₦584,620 materials, ₦7,336 labour and ₦15,526 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦810,836 per m.

How much does standard-gauge track UIC60 on concrete sleepers (600mm c/c) incl. ballast, sub-ballast, fastenings and welds cost per m in Nigeria?
