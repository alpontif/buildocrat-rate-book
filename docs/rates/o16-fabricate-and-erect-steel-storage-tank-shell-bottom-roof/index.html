Frequently asked questions

How much does fabricate and erect steel storage tank shell/bottom/roof, per tonne of plate cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦2,466,956 per tonne net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦3,292,762 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Low-hydrogen electrode E7018, Steel plate S275 for storage tanks) with waste allowances; labour (Rigger / banksman, Unskilled labourer, Welder / fabricator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t, Welding generator 400A (diesel)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Coating, NDT and foundation excluded.
