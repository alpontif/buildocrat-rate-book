Frequently asked questions

How much does high-yield reinforcement 20-25mm, cut, bent & fixed cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦1,202,012 per tonne net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦1,604,382 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦1,275,384 per tonne and the tender rate about ₦1,702,315 per tonne.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Binding wire, High-yield rebar 20-25mm) with waste allowances; labour (Iron bender / steel fixer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
