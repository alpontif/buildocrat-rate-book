Frequently asked questions

How much does structural steel UB/UC frame, fabricated, primed & erected cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦2,422,864 per tonne net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦3,233,911 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦2,566,592 per tonne and the tender rate about ₦3,425,750 per tonne.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Bolt & nut M16x100 grade 8.8 with washers, Cutting/grinding disc 9", Red oxide anti-rust primer, Structural steel sections (UB/UC/PFC/angle), Welding electrodes E6013 2.5mm) with waste allowances; labour (Steel erector, Unskilled labourer, Welder / fabricator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t, Welding generator 400A (diesel)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
