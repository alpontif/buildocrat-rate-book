Structural steel UB/UC frame, fabricated, primed & erected: cost per tonne in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for structural steel UB/UC frame, fabricated, primed & erected is ₦2,428,012 per tonne in Abuja: ₦1,595,190 materials, ₦221,062 labour and ₦611,760 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦3,240,782 per tonne.

Frequently asked questions

How much does structural steel UB/UC frame, fabricated, primed & erected cost per tonne in Nigeria?

About ₦2,428,012 per tonne net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦3,240,782 per tonne as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦2,571,997 per tonne and the tender rate about ₦3,432,965 per tonne.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Bolt & nut M16x100 grade 8.8 with washers, Cutting/grinding disc 9", Red oxide anti-rust primer, Structural steel sections (UB/UC/PFC/angle), Welding electrodes E6013 2.5mm) with waste allowances; labour (Steel erector, Unskilled labourer, Welder / fabricator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Mobile crane 25t, Welding generator 400A (diesel)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
