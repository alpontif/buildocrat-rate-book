Traffic signal installation, 4-way junction: cost per set in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for traffic signal installation, 4-way junction is ₦23,669,525 per set in Abuja: ₦23,337,160 materials, ₦100,215 labour and ₦232,150 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦31,592,824 per set.

Frequently asked questions

How much does traffic signal installation, 4-way junction cost per set in Nigeria?

About ₦23,669,525 per set net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦31,592,824 per set as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦25,089,379 per set and the tender rate about ₦33,487,970 per set.

What does the rate include?

Materials (16mm2 4-core armoured Cu cable, Granite 3/4", OPC 42.5 cement, Sharp sand, Traffic signal set) with waste allowances; labour (Electrician, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Hiab / truck-mounted crane 10t) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
