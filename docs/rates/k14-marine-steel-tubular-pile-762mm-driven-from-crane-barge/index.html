How much does marine steel tubular pile 762mm driven from crane barge cost per m in Nigeria?

What is the rate in Lagos?

What does the rate include?

Materials (Low-hydrogen electrode E7018, Steel tubular pile 762x14mm (258 kg/m)) with waste allowances; labour (Deckhand / marine crew, Piling hand, Welder / fabricator) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Crane barge 300t, Piling rig with diesel/hydraulic impact hammer (crawler), Tug 1,200hp) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
