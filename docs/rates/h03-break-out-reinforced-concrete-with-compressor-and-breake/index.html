Frequently asked questions

How much does break out reinforced concrete with compressor & breakers cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦60,520 per m³ net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦80,779 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦63,821 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦85,185 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (no materials) with waste allowances; labour (Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Air compressor 375cfm with 2 breakers) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
