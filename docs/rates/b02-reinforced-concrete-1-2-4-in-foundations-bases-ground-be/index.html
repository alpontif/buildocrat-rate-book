Frequently asked questions

How much does reinforced concrete 1:2:4 in foundations / bases / ground beams cost per m³ in Nigeria?

About ₦127,692 per m³ net in Abuja as at 6 October 2026, or about ₦170,436 per m³ as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦135,558 per m³ and the tender rate about ₦180,935 per m³.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Granite 3/4", OPC 42.5 cement, Sharp sand, Water) with waste allowances; labour (Mason / bricklayer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Concrete mixer, 1-bag (petrol), Poker vibrator (petrol)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Excl. rebar & formwork. Many QSs allow 6.5-7 bags/m3.
