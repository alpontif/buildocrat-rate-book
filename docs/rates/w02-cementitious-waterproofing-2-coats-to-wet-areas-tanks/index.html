Cementitious waterproofing, 2 coats, to wet areas/tanks: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for cementitious waterproofing, 2 coats, to wet areas/tanks is ₦7,874 per m² in Abuja: ₦7,219 materials, ₦655 labour and ₦0 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦10,509 per m².
