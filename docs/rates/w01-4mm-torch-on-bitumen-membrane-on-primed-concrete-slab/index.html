4mm torch-on bitumen membrane on primed concrete slab: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for 4mm torch-on bitumen membrane on primed concrete slab is ₦7,564 per m² in Abuja: ₦6,418 materials, ₦1,146 labour and ₦0 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦10,096 per m².
