Lightning protection system for a typical 2-storey building (6 air terminals): cost per unit in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for lightning protection system for a typical 2-storey building (6 air terminals) is ₦820,545 per unit in Abuja: ₦729,500 materials, ₦91,045 labour and ₦0 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦1,095,220 per unit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does lightning protection system for a typical 2-storey building (6 air terminals) cost per unit in Nigeria?

About ₦820,545 per unit net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦1,095,220 per unit as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦871,599 per unit and the tender rate about ₦1,163,364 per unit.

What does the rate include?

Materials (Copper tape 25x3mm (earthing/lightning), Copper-bonded earth rod 1.2m with clamp, Lightning air terminal with base, Test clamp (lightning down conductor)) with waste allowances; labour (Electrician, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (no plant) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate.
