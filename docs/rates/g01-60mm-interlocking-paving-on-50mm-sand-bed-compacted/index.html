60mm interlocking paving on 50mm sand bed, compacted: cost per m² in Nigeria

As at 29 September 2026, the net unit rate for 60mm interlocking paving on 50mm sand bed, compacted is ₦11,166 per m² in Abuja: ₦9,534 materials, ₦1,433 labour and ₦200 plant. With overheads (7.5%), contingency (5%), profit (10%) and VAT (7.5%) the tender rate is about ₦14,904 per m².

Frequently asked questions

How much does 60mm interlocking paving on 50mm sand bed, compacted cost per m² in Nigeria?

About ₦11,166 per m² net in Abuja as at 29 September 2026, or about ₦14,904 per m² as a tender rate including overheads, contingency, profit and VAT (Buildocrat Nigeria Construction Rate Book).

What is the rate in Lagos?

Applying Buildocrat's Lagos location factors, the net rate is about ₦11,863 per m² and the tender rate about ₦15,834 per m².

What does the rate include?

Materials (Interlocking paving stone 60mm, Sharp sand, Stone dust) with waste allowances; labour (Interlock/paving layer, Unskilled labourer) at all-in rates including feeding, transport, idle time and statutory costs; and plant (Plate compactor (petrol)) including fuel and operator. Overheads, profit and VAT are excluded from the net rate. Scope note: Sub-base measured separately.
