Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire an excavator per day in Nigeria?

A 20-22 tonne excavator (CAT 320 class) hires for about ₦480,000 a day dry in Abuja as at 6 October 2026. With about 150 litres of diesel and an operator, the all-in cost is about ₦827,850 a day. Mobilisation by low-bed is extra.

How much is bulldozer hire per day in Nigeria?

A D7-class bulldozer is about ₦550,000 a day dry, or about ₦1,044,460 all-in with fuel and operator.

How much is a backhoe (JCB) per day?

About ₦310,000 a day dry, about ₦493,290 all-in.

How much is a 25-tonne crane per day?

About ₦450,000 a day dry, about ₦749,240 all-in.

Why are Buildocrat's all-in plant rates higher than hire quotes?

Hire quotes are often dry (machine only). The all-in rate adds diesel at the current pump price, lubricants and an operator, which is what the work actually costs.
