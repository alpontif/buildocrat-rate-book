Privacy notice

Buildocrat uses privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which prices and tools people use, so we can improve the rate book. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Pages visited, how visitors arrived (for example from Google or an AI assistant), approximate location (country or city), device and browser type.
  • Actions such as opening a rate build-up, adding a line to the estimator, downloading data or tapping the WhatsApp button.
  • Estimator lines and your chosen location are saved only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number or email on this site. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your details only to reply.

Your choices

You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or an ad-blocker; the rate book works the same. For questions or requests under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, contact buildocrat@gmail.com.
